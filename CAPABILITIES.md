# vega-governance-jacket-sdk — Capability Inventory

Each capability below is a real, testable property of the published code. The "why policy fails" column explains why a prompt-level, policy-based, or model-self-check approach structurally cannot provide this guarantee — authority held outside the model cannot be replicated by prompt-level policy (WHITEPAPER.md section 1). Every claim traces to a module and a test file.

## This repo's part in the family predictive loop

The governance jacket sits at the **human-authority end** of the loop described in ORGANISM_MAP.md:

```
records (custody) → lessons (librarian) → knowledge graph (dkg-lattice) → lattice foresight (reasoning/galaxy) → human decision → [THIS REPO: single-use commitment + gates] → action → new records
```

- **What this repo receives**: The human's decision, informed by the lattice's foresight (forecasts, convergence, futures above the present). The human signs a commitment for one exact action.
- **What this repo enforces**: That commitment is single-use, bound to exact parameters, capped at 15 minutes, and checked by gates that fail closed. The model never holds the signing key; authority is non-transitive.
- **What this repo produces**: A minted commitment in the ledger, an exit record in the DAG, and the action's effect on the world. These become new records that feed back into custody → librarian → dkg-lattice → reasoning → galaxy → human.

The jacket is the **authority bottleneck** — the only place where human intent becomes authorized action. Everything above it (reasoning, galaxy, council) informs the human. Everything below it (custody, two-gate, harness) executes and records under that authority. The loop closes when the new records return to the human via the feed/galaxy, and the human decides again.
